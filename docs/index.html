One tap to find TVs

Farly looks for televisions on the Wi-Fi you are on. If one hides, add it by address.

Works with your TV

Roku, Samsung, LG webOS and Android TV / Google TV, controlled over your own network.

A full remote

D-pad, volume, playback, home, back, input and a keyboard for TV search fields.

Mac menu bar

A small companion in your Mac menu bar. Import your saved TVs from your phone.

Stays on your Wi-Fi

Commands go straight from your device to the TV. There is no Farly server and no account.

Open source

MIT licensed. Read the code, file an issue, or send a fix.
