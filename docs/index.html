Your phone
is the remote.

One tap scans your Wi-Fi for TVs, then opens a remote. No account, no cloud, free and open source.

Join the iPhone beta View the source

Now on TestFlight for iPhone. Mac coming soon to TestFlight.

One tap to find TVs

Farly looks for televisions on the Wi-Fi you are on. If one hides, add it by address.

Works with your TV

Roku, Samsung, LG webOS and Android TV / Google TV, controlled over your own network.

A full remote

D-pad, volume, playback, home, back, input and a keyboard for TV search fields.

Mac menu bar

A small companion in your Mac menu bar. Import your saved TVs from your phone. Coming soon to TestFlight.

Stays on your Wi-Fi

Commands go straight from your device to the TV. There is no Farly server and no account.

Open source

MIT licensed. Read the code, file an issue, or send a fix.
